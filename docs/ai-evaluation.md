# AI layer: detector evaluation

Generated by `scripts/evaluate_detectors.py` (`make evaluate`). Do not edit by hand.

**This is simulated data, not real mill data.** We have no months of real logs from Everest, so `scripts/simulate.py` replays 30 days of mill life through the real services and plants five known problems (`docs/simulation.md`). The detectors do not know what was planted. They learn each machine's, supplier's and shift's baseline from the same simulated rows, then this script checks what they flagged against the planted list.

Run: seed 42, 30 days ending 2026-10-10T06:00:00+00:00. Simulation took 55 s, all detectors 0.24 s.

Precision = flagged findings that point at a planted problem. Recall = planted problems found. `n/a` = nothing planted or nothing flagged. A detector with nothing planted is still scored on false alarms: it should stay silent on a normal month.

How findings are matched:

- `supplier_defects`: supplier and defect type equal SLY, colour mismatch.
- `machine_slowdown`: LM-03, and the flagged days overlap the planted decline days.
- `shift_change_idle`: the 06:00 handover (shift C to A).
- `yarn_yield_gap`: the planted order, or a loom whose flagged jobs include its jobs. Order and loom findings count as one planted problem for recall.
- `impossible_data`: machine and timestamp equal a planted late counter log.

Not covered by the planted month: `defect_root_cause` needs a sudden rise in defects and `breakdown_cluster` needs repeated breakdowns; the simulator plants neither. Their recall is shown only by unit tests on hand-built fixtures (`backend/tests/test_analytics_detectors.py`). Here they are checked for false alarms only.
